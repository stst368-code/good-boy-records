A song cover, in a different style, always gets my attention.

Through the magic of YouTube recommendations, I was targeted by AI covers in 2024.
They were bad, but entertaining.

I kept my eyes on them as AI sunk deeper and deeper into life. Slowly but surely, it
became harder and harder to spot an AI singer from a human.

June 2025, the idea materialised in my head, the perfect gift for my musically inclined
Dad, who is a challenge to buy for... an actual CD, burned with all his favourite tracks
ruined by yours truly.

My research began, four months and several thousand generated songs later, here we are at
Good Boy Records, with some confidence I can state that what you see here is the cutting 
edge of what's available at the time of writing this.

I can equally, confidently, state that six months from now this will look like a
dog playing a recorder, so enjoy it whilst it's good.
