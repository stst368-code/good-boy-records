Bundled MP3 files are the authors' public high-quality previews. The library
decodes them to 24-bit WAV, trims leading/trailing silence, and adds a 3 ms
fade at the start. No download or account is required when using these clips in GBR.
The original source pages, authors and licences also accompany the sound-library
entries. Older synthetic presets remain available to projects that already use
them; new projects use these recordings.

Vinyl surface, deep impact and noise riser are original GBR synthesis.
