Good Boy Records is run out of a converted mill in Derbyshire by two golden
retrievers with strong opinions about seating arrangements and a passion for
chasing balls beyond the gate.

On the left side is the cassette magazine, it's a 10-track player, click one
it'll load and start playing. Each track has 6 variants, 5 in standardised
genres and a 6th specialty wildcard per track.

You'll find player controls along the right and further info about the playing 
track available via buttons around the artwork.

This page documents how the music is made and acts as a general introduction to
AI, generative models and diffusion as well as a deep dive into data analysis
and presentation.

Whilst we are two good dogs, we do have to warn you: some songs, and some of the
songs linked from this page, contain explicit language or cover subject matter
that may offend.

Please don't play this around your children.

Yes, most of these songs are about my dogs, I'm just not that creative.
