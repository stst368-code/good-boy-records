CHAPTER 01 · THE INSTRUCTIONS

The music starts with words.

The model doesn't hear a band playing. GBR gives it written musical instructions and a starting latent. Those instructions describe what to sing, what it should sound like and how the song is organised.

01 / LYRICS

What should be sung?

Words, ad-libs and vocal cues give the model material for the performance. The words are guidance, not a guarantee of perfect pronunciation.

02 / CAPTION

What should it sound like?

A musical description can request instrumentation, arrangement, vocals, pace, mood and production character. It steers the sound rather than strictly controlling it.

03 / STRUCTURE

How should it unfold?

Markers such as [verse], [chorus] and [bridge] suggest the sequence of sections. You can inspect the actual markers in GBR's published lyrics below.
