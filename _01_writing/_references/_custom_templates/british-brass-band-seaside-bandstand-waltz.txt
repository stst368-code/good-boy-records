Arrangement
Instrument Lifecycle Description (Primary/Secondary Layering):
Primary: The tuba sets the downbeat and tenor horns supply the two light afterbeats. Cornets lead the waltz tune.
Secondary: Flugelhorn fills the sung gaps, euphonium turns around the cadences and a brushed side drum keeps the step light.
Groove & Foundation Progression: Start with a rounded tuba downbeat and two light tenor-horn chords. A cornet pickup establishes the tune and a soft brushed side drum follows the waltz through the first sung phrase. Flugelhorn fills the space between her lines.

On the first duet refrain, bring in the cornet row in thirds and give the euphonium a small rising turn around the final vocal cadence. Pull back to bass, tenor horns and the odd flugelhorn answer for the male solo. Restore the two-part cornet figures when they sing together again.

Leave only the tuba and one horn under the alternating lines, with a breath before each reply. Return with the full waltz accompaniment for the final duet. A short cornet tag and a lightly delayed last chord close the dance.
