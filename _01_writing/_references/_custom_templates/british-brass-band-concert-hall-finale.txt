Arrangement
Instrument Lifecycle Description (Primary/Secondary Layering):
Primary: Use front and back cornet rows, flugelhorn, tenor and baritone horns, euphoniums, trombones and E-flat and B-flat basses.
Secondary: Use timpani on the opening pedal and the larger cadences, and leave room for flugelhorn beneath the quieter voice.
Groove & Foundation Progression: Start with a low tuba pedal and slow timpani strokes. A principal cornet states a rising figure, the back row repeats it and the full band cuts together to expose the first sung entry. Put mellow horns beneath that entry and let euphonium pick out a quiet echo between phrases.

Set the initial choir statement against broad brass chords. In the following band passage, send short cornet figures across both rows and answer them with nimble euphoniums, using bass trombone and timpani only on the cadences. Break that texture cleanly for the next solo vocal lines, with a soft flugelhorn counterline.

Sing the middle episode against sustained horns in triple metre. Leave an exposed breath and use a rising low-brass figure to restore the opening pulse. Bring the mezzo and full choir together for the last refrain, with soprano cornet answering between the lines. Close with two broad full-band chords after the voices release and let the hall decay.
Embellishments, Textures & Spatial FX: Let the last two brass chords decay through the hall after the voices release.
